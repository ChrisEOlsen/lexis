How it works

From download to answers
in three steps.

01

Download & install

Grab the disk image, drag LEXIS into Applications. That's the whole install — no Homebrew, no terminal, no database to configure.

02

Drop in your documents

Create a group and drop in manuals, papers, contracts, scanned files. Indexing is pure text processing — a thousand documents take minutes, not hours.

03

Ask anything

Ask in plain language. LEXIS searches, re-ranks, and writes a grounded answer from the passages it found — with every source one click away.

Under the hood

Classic search, sharpened
with local models.

Most document-chat tools embed everything into vectors and search those. LEXIS uses classic full-text search — the same family of algorithms behind real search engines — and applies models only where they help.

◈

Rewrite

A small local model refines your question and picks meaning-appropriate synonyms before searching.

→
≣

Search

Full-text lexical search over the index. Exact terms — part numbers, codes, names — always match.

→
⇅

Re-rank

A tiny embedding model re-orders candidates by meaning, so the best passage leads.

→
✦

Answer

A local chat model writes the answer from those passages and nothing else. Fully grounded.

The index never needs rebuilding when models change — swap models freely, your index stays valid.

Why Lexis

LEXIS vs. embedding-based RAG.

The standard local RAG recipe embeds every chunk of every document into vectors at ingest time. LEXIS searches by words instead and applies models only at the edges.

LEXIS lexical + models at the edgesEmbedding RAG vectors
Ingest speedWord counting — thousands of passages/secNeural net over every chunk — ~100× slower
Exact termsExact match, always foundCompressed into "meaning" — rare identifiers blur
Pure paraphraseCovered by query rewrite, synonyms & rerankerStrongest case
Changing modelsIndex untouchedVectors are model-specific — re-embed everything
Why this answer?Visible search terms and passagesA similarity score
Index at scalePostgres on diskVector index, typically in RAM

Paraphrase-only questions are the one real advantage vectors hold — and on our benchmarks, the questions where a full vector index would still have helped came to about 2%.

First launch

One download.
Then it's yours.

The installer is ~80 MB. On first launch, LEXIS downloads the models it needs (~5.1 GB) with a progress bar — interrupted downloads resume. Your documents, config, and database live in ~/Library/Application Support/LEXIS/; the app runs its own private database that only your macOS user can reach.

System requirements

  • MacApple Silicon
  • Memory16 GB recommended
  • Disk~11 GB free
  • NetworkOnly for the one-time model download

After setup, LEXIS works fully offline — on a plane, in a vault, anywhere.
