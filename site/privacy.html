Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

The LEXIS app

LEXIS runs entirely on your Mac. Your documents, the search index, the AI models, and the database all live on your machine, in ~/Library/Application Support/LEXIS/.

  • We collect no personal data from the app.
  • The app transmits nothing to us or any third party. There are no accounts, no API keys, no telemetry, and no analytics inside the app.
  • The only network connection the app makes is the one-time download of its AI models on first launch.

This website

This site is a static page. We do not use cookies and we do not run analytics. If that changes, this policy will be updated.
